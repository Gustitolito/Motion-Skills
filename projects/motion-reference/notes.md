# Motion references

Four eight-second studies, each with entrance, reading hold and exit. They are executable starting points, not finished client films.

| Composition         | Brief                             | Primary skill                  | Decision                                                  |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| reference-premium   | Refined product reveal            | studio-smooth-premium          | restrained travel, one hero, long reading hold            |
| reference-promo     | Energetic invitation              | studio-fast-promo              | one emphasized word, short attack, stable CTA             |
| reference-editorial | Local history interview           | studio-institutional-editorial | quiet hierarchy, readable lower third, no bounce          |
| reference-data      | Explain three illustrative stages | studio-data-viz-explainer      | shared zero baseline and labels; no invented factual data |

Render each with `npm run render -- --composition=reference-premium --preset=preview` (substitute ID).
Review entrance, hold and exit at normal speed, then 0.5x and 2x. Check clipping, reading time, hierarchy, frozen holds and complete exits. See docs/validation.md for automated render coverage and its limits.
